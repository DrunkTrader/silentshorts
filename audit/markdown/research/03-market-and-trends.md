# 03 — Market and trends for AI short-form video tools (2025–2026)

Research date: 22 November 2026. Scope: market size, platform rules, India, the clipping economy and technology trends, read for what they mean for premium features in a clipping tool.

**Method.** Searches went through agent-reach (Exa web search) and pages were read with the Jina reader. Exa's free tier rate-limited parallel queries, so the searches ran one after another. The Verge blocked Jina, so Verge claims are cited from search snippets and backed by other outlets. All fetched content was treated as untrusted data (see "Flagged content" at the end).

**Confidence labels.**
- **High**: read on the primary source, such as a platform help page, an official blog, a regulator or a company filing quoted by major press.
- **Medium**: reputable press, or an analyst firm's own landing page, read but not the paid report.
- **Low**: secondary aggregators, SEO-style blogs, vendor marketing, competitor-run "research", or single anecdotes.

**Estimate type.** Market figures are tagged **[Analyst]**, **[Vendor]** (what a company says about itself), **[Third-party estimate]** (for example Sacra) or **[Secondary]** (a figure repeated with no traceable original).

A large share of 2026 web results came from small SEO sites, many of them run by competing tools. They are used only for leads or colour, always marked Low.

## 2. Platform trends

### 2.1 Length and monetization

**YouTube Shorts**
- Shorts can run up to **3 minutes** since 15 Oct 2024 (High — https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/tall-updates-coming-to-shorts/ and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en).
- Shorts over 1 minute that carry an active Content ID claim are blocked (Medium — https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/technology/youtube-shorts-to-be-up-to-three-minutes-long/article68717299.ece).

**YouTube scale and view counting**
- Shorts average **200B daily views** (CEO at Cannes, June 2025; repeated in YouTube's Aug 2026 blog). High — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-reports-200-billion-daily-views-for-shorts-format/549315/ and the YPP blog below.
- From 31 Mar 2025 a Shorts view counts every play or replay. Payouts use "engaged views", and non-original Shorts (unedited clips of others, re-uploads, compilations with nothing added) are **ineligible views**. High — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en and https://www.youtube.com/creators/create/shorts/

**YouTube Partner Program changes from 1 Feb 2027 (biggest monetization change since 2018).** High — https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/ and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en
- To earn each month from the Shorts Creator Pool, a channel needs **10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days**. Channels below that stay in YPP and keep their long-form earnings.
- YPP entry for new creators becomes **8,000 qualified watch hours / 365 days, or 20M qualified Shorts views / 90 days**, plus 1,000 subscribers.
- New Shorts incentives: Shopping bonuses, brand-deal production credits and "trend activation" boosts.
- Targeted Shorts ads aimed at five or fewer channels pay a direct 45% share.
- Shorts keeps its 45% revenue share and long-form its 55%.
- Fan funding and Shopping stay at 500 subscribers with 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views.
- Qualified views exclude loops and views of Shorts shown as ads (Low — https://www.cowral.com/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization-2027).

**TikTok**
- Creator Rewards pays only for videos **at least 1 minute long**. Rewards weigh originality, play duration, search value and engagement. High — https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en and https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en
- 60-minute uploads were tested in 2024 (Medium — https://www.theverge.com/2024/5/16/24158491/tiktok-hour-long-video-uploads-youtube).
- In the US the program is now run by "TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC" (terms updated 20 Jul 2026, High — https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en).

**Instagram**
- Reels can run up to **3 minutes** since 18 Jan 2025, up from 90 s (High — https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/DE-efFqyStv and https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram?locale=en_US).

**Implication.** "Short" now means anything up to 3 minutes, and TikTok's payout program pays only from 1 minute up. A clipper capped at 15–60 s can't produce TikTok-monetizable clips. Long-cut clips (60–180 s) with chapter-style narrative are worth offering as a premium option.

### 2.2 AI-content labelling and disclosure

**YouTube**
- Disclosure is required for *realistic* content that is meaningfully altered or generated. Explicitly exempt:
  - caption creation;
  - AI scripts, titles and thumbnails;
  - upscaling and audio repair;
  - **cloning one's own voice for voice-overs or dubs**;
  - **gameplay footage**.

  High — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- From **May 2026**, YouTube auto-detects and labels content left undisclosed. Labels now sit as an **overlay on Shorts** and directly under the player on long-form.
- Labels can't be removed when the content carries verified C2PA metadata saying it is fully AI-generated.
- YouTube says a label alone doesn't affect recommendations or monetization, but persistent non-disclosure can be penalized.

  High — https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/424874071/updates-to-ai-content-disclosure-and-labels?hl=en, and Medium — https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/05/youtube-to-begin-automatically-labeling-ai-videos/

**Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads)**
- The "AI info" label is applied from industry-standard signals (C2PA/IPTC) or self-disclosure.
- For content that was only *edited* with AI, the label moved into the post menu.

High — https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/

**TikTok**
- Creators must label realistic AIGC, and unlabeled content may be removed or restricted (Community Guidelines effective 24 Sep 2026).
- TikTok was the first video platform to adopt C2PA (2024) and has labelled **more than 5B AI-generated videos**.
- An invisible watermark (VeraDura) and a public checker tool launched in Nov 2026.
- Likeness Detection is rolling out globally.
- An AIGC amount slider sits in Manage Topics (Nov 2025).

High — https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-tools-to-provide-clarity-and-control-around-ai-generated-content?lang=en-GB, https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity and https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/tiktok-now-lets-you-choose-how-much-ai-generated-content-you-want-to-see/

**Regulation**
- **EU AI Act Article 50**: transparency duties apply from **2 Aug 2026**. Generative systems must mark outputs in machine-readable form, and deepfakes must be disclosed. Systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have a marking grace period to Dec 2026 (AI Omnibus). High — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/quick-facts-transparency-rules-ai-systems and https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50
- **India, IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Amendment Rules 2026**: notified 10 Feb 2026, in force **20 Feb 2026**. High — https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/49d4c4b42fc2d3dce00e0fdcf7ed1389.pdf
  - Permitted synthetic content must carry a prominent visible label (an audio prefix for audio-only).
  - It must also carry **permanent embedded metadata or provenance with a unique identifier** where technically feasible.
  - Intermediaries "should not offer 'remove watermark', 'export without metadata', or similar functionalities".
  - Significant social media intermediaries must collect an "AI-generated: Yes/No" declaration and verify it.
  - The takedown window drops from 36 to **3 hours**.
  - Routine good-faith editing and accessibility work are excluded.
  - The Oct 2025 draft's "10% of screen area" label size (Medium — https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/india-proposes-strict-it-rules-labelling-deepfakes-amid-ai-misuse-2025-10-22/) is not in the FAQ language I read; the final text says "prominent".

### 2.3 C2PA / Content Credentials

- **Google Pixel 10** attaches Content Credentials to every Pixel Camera photo, at C2PA Assurance Level 2. Google Photos adds them to AI edits. High — https://blog.google/security/pixel-android-trusted-images-c2pa-content-credentials/
- **YouTube** shows a "Captured with a camera" disclosure for C2PA-signed, unaltered uploads (since Oct 2024). High — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15446725?hl=en
- **LinkedIn** shows a C2PA icon (rolling out, High — https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6282984).
- **TikTok** joined the C2PA Steering Committee on 27 Jul 2026 (High — https://c2pa.org/c2pa-welcomes-tiktok-to-steering-committee/).
- **The gap**: credentials are lost when content is downloaded, re-encoded or edited, which TikTok itself admits (TikTok newsroom, above). A third-party check found each platform describes support differently (Low — https://newwatermark.com/guides/which-platforms-show-content-credentials/).
- **Implication.** A clipping tool that re-encodes source video strips upstream credentials. Signing exports with a C2PA manifest that records "clipped, reframed, captioned, AI-dubbed" is becoming a compliance and trust feature, not decoration. The India and EU rules push the same way for any AI-dubbed or voice-cloned output.

### 2.4 Unoriginal, reused and "inauthentic" content: the biggest risk for clipping tools

**YouTube**
- **15 Jul 2025**: "repetitious content" was renamed **"inauthentic content"** and now covers mass-produced, templated content, including "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates" (High — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en).
- The **reused content** policy bars monetizing others' content "without adding significant original commentary, substantive modifications, or educational or entertainment value" (High, same page).
- YouTube said reaction and commentary channels are not the target (Medium — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-targeting-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/).
- **1 Oct 2026**: Shorts recommendations will "**reduce the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators**" without significant changes, explicitly aimed at clipper and aggregator channels (High — https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts?hl=en-au; Medium — https://www.engadget.com/2275978/youtube-takes-aim-at-clipper-accounts-will-prioritize-original-shorts/ and https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-reposted-clips-reach/591774/).

**TikTok**
- From **24 Sep 2026**, For You feed eligibility excludes reused or unoriginal content "posted without creative edits, such as clips that show someone else's watermark or logo", and minimally edited content. High — https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/fyf-standards, with the quote via Low — https://remuxe.com/articles/tiktok-unoriginal-content

**Meta**
- **Instagram (Apr 2024)**: originals replace copies in recommendations, copies are labelled, and aggregators that repost more than 10 times in 30 days leave recommendations (High — https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality).
- **Facebook (13 Mar 2026)**: "original" redefined. Remixes count only with analysis, discussion or new information, and minor-edit re-uploads are deprioritized (High — https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/).
- **Instagram (30 Apr 2026)**: the same rule extended to photos and carousels (High — https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram).

**Effect on clippers.** Mint reports YouTube's October move "raised questions about whether India's nascent clipping business can scale further" (Medium — https://www.livemint.com/industry/youtubes-new-rules-pose-survival-test-for-india-s-clipping-firms-content-11792129491031.html). A creator-side summary: "clipping isn't dead; copy-paste is" (Low — https://monetiz.space/en/blog/is-clipping-dead/).

**Implication for product.** All three platforms now reward a clip that is the creator's own, or that adds commentary, context, a narrative or a substantive edit, and they penalize near-identical re-uploads and templated mass output. Two kinds of feature gain value:
1. Tools that **add authorship**:
   - voice-over or commentary track;
   - reaction cam;
   - on-screen context and explainer overlays;
   - B-roll;
   - recut narratives built from several moments;
   - hooks grounded in what the clip actually says;
   - style variety so channels don't look templated.
2. A **rights and authorization layer**: an "official clipper" mode where the source owner grants rights, plus stripping or avoiding other creators' watermarks and logos only when the clipper holds the rights.

Pure "paste a URL, get 10 near-identical clips" is now the riskiest workflow on all three platforms. Confidence: High that this is the direction; Medium on how hard enforcement bites.

## 4. The clipping and "clip farming" economy

**Scale and press**
- Clipping went mainstream in 2025:
  - WSJ (Aug 2025): https://www.wsj.com/tech/meet-the-clippers-cashing-in-on-your-social-media-feeds-f21c104a

## 5. Technology trends

### 5.1 Agentic video editing

- a16z's thesis "It's time for agentic video editing" (21 Jan 2026): editing takes about 80% of production time, and vision models plus agents can now plan and run edits (High — https://a16z.com/its-time-for-agentic-video-editing/).

### 5.5 Virality prediction: claims against evidence

- **Best public benchmark**: the VQualA 2025 Engagement Prediction Challenge (ICCV 2025) on 120,651 Snapchat Spotlight videos. The winning ensemble of large multimodal models reached **SROCC 0.707 / PLCC 0.714** against a baseline of about 0.66 (High — https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02969 and https://github.com/sunwei925/LMM-EVQA/tree/main/).
  - It predicts watch-continuation and engagement *ranking*, not whether a clip goes viral.
- Academic work stresses that virality depends on **external trends** rather than content alone (Medium — https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18653v1).
- A rubric-based VLM model explains about 61% of engagement variance on edutainment (R² 0.61, Medium — https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21402v1).
- Leakage-safe TikTok studies show creator influence dominates (Low/Medium — https://www.ijscia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Volume7-Issue6-Nov-Dec-No.1121-983-999.pdf).
- **Vendor scores**: no vendor-published validation of a "virality score" against real outcomes turned up. A competitor found **82% of 7,101 OpusClip clips scored 80 or higher**, a compressed scale useful for sorting but weak for prediction ([Competitor], Low — https://www.scalereach.ai/blog/how-accurate-is-the-opus-clip-virality-score/).
- **Read**: sell a score as *relative ranking within one source video*. The honest premium version is a **feedback loop**: pull real post-performance through platform APIs and recalibrate per channel. Confidence: Medium.

## 6. What this means for premium features (synthesis)

Ranked by impact on a clipping tool:

1. **Originality is the new gate.** YouTube (Jul 2025, Oct 2026), TikTok (Sep 2026) and Meta (Mar and Apr 2026) all cut reach or monetization for re-uploads and templated mass output. Premium features:
   - commentary and voice-over tracks;
   - reaction cam and context overlays;
   - multi-moment recut narratives;
   - per-channel style variation;
   - an owner-authorized "official clipper" mode.
2. **Platform revenue share shrinks for small channels, and paid clipping grows.** YPP from Feb 2027 needs 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days to earn from Shorts; Whop and Content Rewards CPM campaigns sit around US$1 per 1K. Premium features:
   - campaign-brief compliance;
   - bulk varied variants;
   - multi-account scheduling;
   - per-clip view and payout reporting.
3. **Labels and provenance become compliance.** YouTube auto-labels with Shorts overlays (May 2026), EU Art. 50 applies (Aug 2026) and India's SGI rules are in force (Feb 2026). Premium features:
   - C2PA-signed exports recording the edit chain;
   - automatic AI-use disclosure on upload when dubbing or voice cloning is used;
   - no "strip metadata" option for synthetic outputs.
4. **Agent and MCP access is table stakes and its price is falling.** Descript, Kapwing and OpusClip all ship MCP, and OpusClip AI Producer is free in Claude and ChatGPT. The premium lies in deterministic per-job pricing, posting and scheduling through the agent, and result quality.
5. **"Short" is now 1–3 minutes, and dubbing is commoditizing.** TikTok rewards need clips of at least 1 minute, and Shorts and Reels run up to 3 minutes. Premium features:
   - long-cut clip modes;
   - lip-synced, expressive, Hinglish and regional dubs that travel to TikTok and Reels, where YouTube's free auto-dub doesn't reach.

**India.** The opportunity is large and the price point is low:
- 650M+ monthly Shorts viewers;
- 98% of internet users consume Indic-language content;
- 2–2.5M monetized creators;
- local tools priced at about ₹350–800 a month on UPI.

The fitting move is rupee pricing on UPI AutoPay. On top of that, premium value comes from:
- best-in-class Hinglish and regional captions (Latin or native script, at the user's choice);
- Hindi and regional dubbing;
- built-in compliance with India's SGI labelling;
- workflows for Indian clipping agencies, news and microdrama clipping, which the Oct 2026 YouTube change pushes toward value-added edits.
